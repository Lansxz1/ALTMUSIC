FAQs

Quick answers to the most common questions about altmusic.

What is altmusic?

altmusic is a music discovery platform for alternative sounds. Browse new songs, explore artists and playlists, and find music for every mood, all in one place.

How do I start listening?

Select Start listening on the Home page or visit the Library to browse songs, playlists, and artists.

Can artists share their music?

We love hearing from artists. Send us a message on the Contact Us page with a link to your music and we will be in touch.

How do I contact you?

Use the form on the Contact Us page and we will reply by email.
