About altmusic

altmusic is a music discovery platform made for people who love alternative sounds. We bring together new releases, standout artists, and playlists for every mood, so finding your next favorite song is easy.

We started with a simple idea: great music should be easy to find and a pleasure to listen to. Too often, discovering new songs means digging through crowded apps and endless menus. altmusic is built to be different, with a clean, simple design that keeps the focus on the music.

Alternative music is about more than a single genre. It is about artists who try something new, songs that feel personal, and sounds that stay with you long after they end. From mellow late-night playlists to high-energy favorites, we want every listener to find something that fits the moment.

On altmusic, you can browse featured tracks on the Home page, explore playlists and artists in the Library, and find answers to common questions in our FAQs. If you want an even better experience, Premium gives you ad-free listening, your own playlists, and more ways to discover music you love.

We also believe in supporting the artists behind the music. Every song starts with someone's creativity, and we are committed to helping listeners connect with the people who make it.

Thank you for being part of altmusic. Whether you are here for a quick listen or a long discovery session, we hope you find something new to love. Have a question or an idea for us? Visit the Contact Us page, because we would love to hear from you.
